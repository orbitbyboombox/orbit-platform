# ORBIT v1 Scope

ORBIT v1.0 incluye Dashboard/Command Center, Customers, Events, Reservation Pipeline manual y automático, cotizaciones, contratos PDF, Portal Cliente, Payment Ledger, Accounts Receivable, Staff, Staff Portal, Operations Center, Assignment Engine, Event Settlement, Payroll, costos operacionales, Cost Master, Branding, Finance Read Model, Accounts Payable, Cash Flow, Profitability, Reports, Resources, Founder Workspace, Settings, System Health, Google Workspace y BOOMBOX Academy.

Quedan fuera de v1.0 nuevas funciones v1.1/v1.2/v2.0 y las mejoras P2/P3 registradas en Roadmap. v1.0 está cerrada y sus módulos certificados solo admiten bugs críticos de Production.
