# BOOMBOX® logo source

The canonical white BOOMBOX® master is `public/branding/boombox-logo-official-white.png`. It is the original approved 2048 × 806 transparent PNG supplied by the Founder. Keep this file unchanged and use it as the source for white BOOMBOX® marks in ORBIT.

For email headers, use `public/branding/boombox-logo-official-white-email.png`. It is an exact crop to the visible alpha bounds of the master, retaining the original white artwork and registered mark at 1563 × 356. The Finance Staff Q1 and Q2 templates load this derivative from `https://app.bbox.cl/branding/boombox-logo-official-white-email.png`.

Other BOOMBOX and partner logos remain available for their existing uses.
